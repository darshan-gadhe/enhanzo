## Why this is not keyword-stuffed

Play's "Store Listing and Promotion" policy prohibits repetitive or irrelevant
keywords. Listings that stuff them get suppressed or rejected. Every term above
appears in a sentence that describes something the app actually does, which is
both what the policy requires and what converts a store visit into an install.

The high-value terms are placed in the first two lines, because that is what Play
indexes most strongly and what a user sees before tapping "Read more".

## After you publish

Play Console → Store performance → **Search terms** shows the queries you are
actually being found for. Rewrite the short description around whichever of these
terms show impressions but low conversion — that is where ASO gains come from,
not from adding more words.
